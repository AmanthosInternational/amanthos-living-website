2. Overview of Data Processing

We take the protection of your personal data very seriously. We treat your personal data confidentially and in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), applicable cantonal data protection regulations, and this privacy policy.

When you use this website, various personal data may be collected. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

3. Data Collection on This Website

3.1 Server Log Files

Our hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files, which your browser transmits to us. These include:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referrer URL
  • Hostname of the accessing device
  • Time of the server request
  • IP address (anonymised)

This data is not merged with other data sources. The basis for data processing is our legitimate interest in ensuring the secure and efficient operation of our website.

3.2 Cookies and Local Storage

This website uses essential cookies for functionality. We also use localStorage to save your language preference and your analytics choice. In addition, Google Analytics 4 (section 4.1) sets the analytics cookies _ga and _ga_<container ID>. No advertising cookies are set. When you first visit our site, a consent banner lets you accept or decline analytics; you can change that choice at any time (section 4.1).

3.3 Contact and Booking Forms

When you contact us via email or use our booking form, the data you provide (name, email address, phone number, booking details) will be stored and processed for the purpose of handling your inquiry or booking. We will not share this data with third parties without your consent, unless required for the fulfilment of your booking (e.g. with our property management system).

4. Analytics and Monitoring

4.1 Google Analytics 4

This website uses Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. We use it to measure reach and to understand how the site is used. For this purpose Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<container ID> and processes information about your visit, including a shortened IP address. Data retention is set to 14 months.

Advertising features and Google Signals are switched off, and no advertising cookies are set. This property is not linked to any advertising account.

Google Analytics runs with Consent Mode v2. If you visit us from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, no analytics data is collected until you accept in our consent banner. From all other countries, including Switzerland, measurement is based on our legitimate interest in understanding how our website is used; you can object at any time, and your objection takes effect immediately and permanently on this device.

You can change your choice here at any time: Change analytics settings.

During the booking process we transmit to Google Analytics the same steps we send to Plausible, and in addition the room type and rate name, the number of adults, the total amount of a confirmed booking and the booking number as the transaction reference. Names, email addresses, telephone numbers, travel dates, the number of children, voucher codes and error messages are not transmitted.

4.2 Plausible Analytics

To measure how the site is used, we also use Plausible Analytics, provided by Plausible Insights OÜ (Estonia). Plausible works without cookies and without cross-device recognition. No personal data and no complete IP addresses are stored; evaluation is carried out exclusively in aggregated form.

During the booking process we additionally count individual steps, such as the availability search, viewing offers and completing a booking. What is transmitted is the property concerned, the respective step, and, for a confirmed booking, its total amount. Booking and reservation numbers, names, email addresses, travel dates, guest numbers and voucher codes are not transmitted.

We also count clicks on links to other websites, file downloads, form submissions and views of an error page. What is transmitted is the target address of the link or file and the path of the error page. The contents of the forms are not transmitted.

4.3 Error and Performance Monitoring

This website uses Sentry, a service provided by Functional Software, Inc., to detect technical errors and measure loading performance. Processing takes place in the provider's EU region. We collect error messages with technical details about browser and device, as well as loading speed metrics. IP addresses, cookies and form contents are not transmitted.

If an error occurs, the course of the affected session is additionally recorded so that the cause can be traced. Text, input and image content are fully masked in the process. No recording takes place without an error.

5. Booking and Payment

When you make a booking through our website, we collect the information necessary to process your reservation, including:

  • Name and contact details
  • Check-in and check-out dates
  • Number of guests
  • Payment information (processed securely via our payment provider)

Payment transactions are handled by third-party payment processors. We do not store your full payment card details on our servers.

6. Your Rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: You can request information about your stored personal data at any time.
  • Right to rectification: You can request the correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to deletion: You can request the deletion of your personal data, unless we are legally obligated to retain it.
  • Right to data portability: You can request that we transfer your data to you or another controller in a commonly used format.
  • Right to object: You can object to the processing of your personal data at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@amanthosliving.com.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website uses SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmissions.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: For displaying web fonts. Google may collect your IP address when fonts are loaded. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics 4): Audience and usage measurement, with consent in the EEA and the UK. See section 4 above.
  • Plausible Insights OÜ (Estonia): Cookie-free audience measurement. See section 4 above.
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry): Error and performance monitoring, processed in the EU region. See section 4 above.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this privacy policy, or as required by law (e.g. tax and accounting obligations). Booking data is retained for the duration required by Swiss law.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
